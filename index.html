How it works

  1. Open

    Choose a PDF or a photo from your phone or computer.

  2. Retype

    Tap any line and change it. Sign, fill forms, reorder pages or black things out.

  3. Save or share

    Download the new copy, or send it straight from your phone.

Questions

Does the new text look like the old text?

Unchanged letters keep their original shapes. New letters use the font inside the PDF when it has them, and the closest free font when it does not.

Is it free? Is there a watermark?

It is free, with no account, no page limit and no watermark.

Where does my file go?

Nowhere. The editor runs in your browser and reads the file on your device. Fonts and text recognition data are downloaded when needed.

What are the limits?

Text does not reflow, so a longer line can run into what follows. On scans, changed words are covered, not erased.

Can I edit a signed PDF?

Yes, but the signature will no longer be valid, and PDFEdit says so. Only edit documents you are allowed to change.
